Protect Privacy

One click. Everything private, found.

MikiViewer reads the whole image, outlines what it finds in red, and hides the kinds you keep ticked: faces blurred, text under solid black bars that can’t be read back.

01 — Faces

Every face, even the small ones in a crowd.

Large photos are searched tile by tile, so group shots and collages come back with every face covered.

02 — Cards

Card numbers, checked like a bank would.

Each number is verified with the card checksum, so random digits are left alone. Numbers printed in spaced groups are caught too, along with expiry dates and CVV codes.

03 — Screenshots

Passwords, keys and addresses in one pass.

Names, dates, IP addresses, passwords, API keys, file paths, phone numbers and emails, all hidden while the labels stay readable.

On your Mac

Nothing leaves your computer. Not even to check.

Read and hidden on-deviceText recognition and redaction run on your Mac with Apple Vision and Tesseract. No image is ever uploaded.
No account, works offlineOpen the app and use it. The only download is an extra OCR language, and only when you ask for one.
Saved files stay cleanHidden pixels are kept in memory only so Show Again can work. A saved file never contains them, and location data can be stripped too.

Installing

  1. Open MikiViewer.pkg and follow the steps. Any previous version is removed first; your languages and settings are kept.
  2. MikiViewer isn’t notarized by Apple, so macOS blocks the first launch. Right-click the app and choose Open, or allow it in System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security.

Prefer the disk image?

Open MikiViewer.dmg and drag MikiViewer onto Applications, choosing Replace if asked.
